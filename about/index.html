Our Story

A Local HVAC Team That Knows Georgetown Homes

Georgetown Heating & Cooling was built to serve the homes and families of Halton Hills and the surrounding Wellington County communities we call home.

Georgetown is a town of contrasts. Stroll Main Street South and you'll find century homes with thick stone foundations and original boiler conversions sitting just blocks from raised ranches, split-levels, and brand-new builds pushing up toward the Niagara Escarpment. That mix of housing stock means there is no single HVAC answer here. A high-efficiency furnace that runs perfectly in a 1990s build on Maple Avenue can short-cycle all winter in an older home on Mill Street with single-pane windows and imperfect insulation. We start every job by understanding the house in front of us, not by pushing a one-size-fits-all package.

Our technicians live locally and drive these roads daily, from Acton and Limehouse through Norval and into Fergus, Elora, and Rockwood. We know which neighborhoods sit low and hold the cold, which properties near the Credit River deal with humidity that strains air conditioners every July, and which exposed driveways off Highway 7 leave furnaces working overtime in February. That local knowledge shapes how we diagnose, install, and tune every system.

Being family-run means we answer for the work long after the truck pulls away. When a furnace quits on a sub-zero night or an AC struggles through a humid Grand River valley summer, our neighbours call us because they know the job will be done right, priced honestly, and explained clearly the first time.

Built for Georgetown's Real Seasons

Lake-effect cold off Lake Ontario in January, sticky humidity rolling up the Credit River valley in July, and the wind that funnels along the Escarpment in between, our climate tests heating and cooling equipment hard. We size, install, and service furnaces, central air, heat pumps, ductless systems, thermostats, and ductwork with those local conditions in mind, so your home stays comfortable no matter what the Ontario weather throws at it.

Our Commitment

Honest Work, Clear Pricing, Year-Round Reliability

Our commitment is straightforward: show up when promised, diagnose carefully, and never sell a system a home doesn't need. Whether we're installing a ductless mini-split in a converted Acton barn, replacing a tired thermostat in a Georgetown starter home, or cleaning ductwork that hasn't been touched since the original owners moved in, the standard never changes. We service every brand, recommend high-efficiency upgrades only when the math genuinely favors them, and back our labour with straightforward warranties.

We also believe comfort should be planned, not panicked. That's why we push twice-yearly maintenance, spring for cooling and fall for heating, so a small worn part gets caught in October instead of becoming a no-heat emergency in January. For customers across Georgetown, Milton, Guelph, and the Wellington County communities in between, that proactive approach has meant fewer breakdowns, lower energy bills, and heating and cooling equipment that lasts the way it was designed to.
